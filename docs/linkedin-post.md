## MAIN POST

2,389 projects. 328,469 units. Since 2017.

That's what Jaipur's RERA register holds — and I've just read all of it.

A few things stood out:

🏗️ What's being built — 1,248 Group Housing projects against 1,037 plotted layouts
✅ What's finished — 1,155 projects (48%) completed, 747 still in progress
⚠️ What stalled — 421 on the authority's own Lapsed list, about 18% of the register
⏳ What slipped — 460 projects (19%) hold at least one extension. The median extension is a full year; the longest single project carries six of them, totalling just over ten years

And where you are changes what gets built:
📍 302017 — 113 Group Housing projects, 13 plotted
📍 303904 — 4 Group Housing, 68 plotted

Two things I'd rather say than hide:

• "Lapsed" has two official definitions on the portal and they disagree — 421 projects on the Lapsed list, 400 in the per-project status field. The deck quotes both, and says which is which.
• A pincode is a postal boundary, not a planning one. I've used it only as the address geography the portal itself records, which it does for 2,387 of the 2,389 projects.

Everything comes from the Rajasthan RERA portal's own register and project pages. Nothing is estimated.
